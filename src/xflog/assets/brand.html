The mark

Two paths cross, then continue. A small break keeps the paths legible and makes the intersection the point of interest. The mark works without color, from a browser tab to a full sheet.

Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark’s width on every side. Minimum mark size: 20px; favicon exception: 16px. Never stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects.

The voice

Calm, precise, human. “Followed you.” “Tracking paused.” “Provider time not supplied.” Say what happened and what is known.

Never imply surveillance, blame, a complete follower count, or guaranteed recovery. A follower event is a change; a paid subscription is a separate relationship.

Move with a reason.

Quick feedback. A quiet arrival. No endless performance. 140 / 240 / 360ms with a single decelerating curve. Reduced motion is always respected.
